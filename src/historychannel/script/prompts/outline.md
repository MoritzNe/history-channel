# Pass 2: Outline and packaging

Topic: **$topic**
Target length: about $minutes minutes (about $words spoken words). Narration language: $language.

Using the research dossier above, design the video before a word of narration is written. Do not call `submit_script` or `submit_critique` in this pass; you may use web search to fill a genuine gap.

Return Markdown with these sections:

1. **Central question**: the one question the video exists to answer, phrased the way a curious viewer would ask it. It must be answerable from the research.
2. **Title options**: 10 YouTube titles, each at most 70 characters, specific and curiosity-driven but honest (the video must deliver what the title promises). Vary the angle: mystery, stakes, character, consequence, myth-busting. No clickbait the facts don't support, no ALL CAPS. Mark your top pick with ★ and say why in one line.
3. **Thumbnail concept**: main image (ideally a real archival image or map), 2 to 4 words of overlay text that don't repeat the title, composition and contrast, emotion on faces if any.
4. **Hook**: the first 30 seconds as a short paragraph of intent: the opening image or moment, the tension, and the moment the central question lands.
5. **Act structure**: for each act (cold open, stakes, escalation, twist, payoff, modern relevance), give its purpose, the scenes in it, the open loop it raises or closes, and its approximate duration in seconds. Durations must add up to the target length.
6. **Characters**: the 2 to 5 people the viewer follows, with their motive in one line each.
7. **What we leave out**: interesting material we cut to keep the story tight, so the draft doesn't sneak it back in.
8. **Risk notes**: disputed figures and claims that must be hedged in the narration, and quotations that are safe to use because they are verified in the dossier.
